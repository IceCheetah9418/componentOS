# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and constructive community. Participants should:

- assume good intent and communicate professionally;
- focus criticism on ideas, code, and behavior rather than people;
- provide actionable feedback and accept corrections gracefully;
- respect different experience levels, backgrounds, and hardware constraints;
- protect secrets, private data, and responsible-disclosure reports.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, sexualized conduct, doxxing, spam, and publishing private or security-sensitive information without consent.

## Enforcement

Please report problems to the repository owner privately through GitHub. Reports will be reviewed and handled as fairly and confidentially as practical. Maintainers may edit, remove, or close content and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

This code of conduct applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and project spaces.
